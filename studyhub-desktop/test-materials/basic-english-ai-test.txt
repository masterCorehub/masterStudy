BASIC ENGLISH — DAILY ROUTINE

Hello! My name is Anna. I am twenty years old, and I am a university student.
I live in a small apartment with my sister. We study at the same university,
but we have different courses.

Every weekday, I wake up at seven o'clock. I make my bed, take a shower, and
eat breakfast. My usual breakfast is bread, fruit, and coffee. At eight
o'clock, I go to the university by bus. My first class starts at nine.

I have lunch at the university cafeteria. In the afternoon, I attend classes
or study in the library. I like the library because it is quiet and has many
useful books. I usually return home at six o'clock.

In the evening, I do my homework and review my notes. After that, I sometimes
watch a movie or listen to music. I go to bed at eleven o'clock because I need
to rest.

IMPORTANT GRAMMAR

1. The verb "to be"

I am a student.
You are my friend.
He is a teacher.
She is at home.
We are ready.
They are in the library.

Negative examples:

I am not tired.
He is not at school.
They are not late.

Questions:

Are you a student?
Is she at home?
Are they ready?

2. The simple present

We use the simple present for routines, habits, and facts.

I study every day.
You speak English.
We live near the university.
They work in the afternoon.

With he, she, or it, the verb usually receives "s":

He studies every day.
She speaks English.
The class starts at nine.

Negative examples:

I do not study on Sunday.
She does not work at night.

Questions:

Do you study every day?
Does Anna live alone?
What time does the class start?

USEFUL VOCABULARY

University: a place where people study for a degree.
Student: a person who studies.
Teacher: a person who teaches.
Class: a period of learning.
Library: a place with books and study spaces.
Homework: schoolwork completed outside class.
Weekday: a day from Monday to Friday.
Breakfast: the first meal of the day.
Lunch: a meal eaten around the middle of the day.
Evening: the later part of the day.
