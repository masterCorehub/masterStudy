# CampusFlow - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Executive Summary
**CampusFlow** is an AI-native academic management ecosystem designed to transform the chaotic university experience into a structured, high-performance journey. By centralizing tasks, notes, scheduling, and performance tracking into a "Deep Focus" environment, CampusFlow reduces cognitive load and empowers students to achieve academic excellence with less stress.

## 2. Problem Statement
Contemporary students navigate a fragmented digital landscape:
- **Information Silos:** Materials are scattered across LMS (Moodle/Canvas), email, and messaging apps.
- **Cognitive Overload:** Constant switching between tools for planning, studying, and tracking leads to "context switching" fatigue.
- **Uncertainty:** Lack of real-time visibility into grade requirements and attendance safety margins.
- **Active Learning Gap:** Static notes often fail to transition into long-term memory.

## 3. Core Personas
- **The Overwhelmed Achiever (Ana):** Needs maximum efficiency in limited study windows.
- **The Grade-Conscious Strategist (Lucas):** Needs predictive analytics to manage academic risk.
- **The Organized Planner (Carla):** Values aesthetic consistency and centralized project management.

## 4. Functional Requirements

### 4.1. Core Workspace (Dashboard)
- **Centralized View:** Daily schedule, upcoming deadlines, and current focus session.
- **Navigation:** Seamless access to Academic, Knowledge, and Performance modules.

### 4.2. Academic Management
- **Course Management:** Dedicated pages for each discipline including faculty info, syllabus, and localized resources.
- **Smart Calendar:** Integration of class schedules, exam dates, and personal study blocks.
- **Task Management:** Dual-view (List/Kanban) task tracking with priority levels and sub-task breakdowns.
- **Template Library:** Pre-formatted structures for ABNT, IEEE, and technical reports to bypass "blank page" syndrome.

### 4.3. Knowledge & Focus Tools
- **Interactive Whiteboard (Canvas):** A free-form space for mind mapping and diagramming.
- **AI-Powered Editor:** Distraction-free writing environment with integrated AI suggestions for summaries and flashcard generation.
- **Immersion Mode:** A high-focus UI with floating window widgets for video lectures, PDFs, and timers.
- **Pomodoro Timer:** Integrated focus sessions with productivity logging.
- **AI Flashcards:** Automatic conversion of notes and whiteboard diagrams into Spaced Repetition System (SRS) decks.

### 4.4. Performance Intelligence
- **Grade Predictor:** Simulation of required final exam scores based on current performance.
- **Attendance Monitor:** "Safe margin" alerts to prevent failure by absence.
- **Focus Heatmap:** Data visualization of peak productivity hours based on session history.

## 5. Visual Identity (Deep Focus)
- **Philosophy:** Minimalist, high-density whitespace, reduced visual noise.
- **Palette:** 
  - Primary: Deep Navy (#1E293B) for authority.
  - Accent: Royal Blue (#2563EB) for interactivity.
  - Background: Soft Gray (#F8FAFC) to reduce eye strain.
- **Typography:** Inter (Sans-Serif) for clarity and modern technical feel.
- **Components:** Rounded-eight (8px) corners, subtle elevations, and consistent iconographies.

## 6. Technical Architecture & Integrations
- **Cloud Sync:** Cross-device persistence (Desktop/Mobile).
- **Integrations:** GitHub (Code repos), Google Drive (Storage), Overleaf (LaTeX), and university LMS connectors.
- **AI Layer:** Local and LLM-based processing for text analysis, diagram extraction, and predictive modeling.

## 7. Success Metrics
- **Retention:** User engagement with the SRS (Flashcard) system.
- **Efficiency:** Reduction in time spent on administrative planning vs. actual study.
- **Performance:** User-reported improvement in GPA and attendance management.
